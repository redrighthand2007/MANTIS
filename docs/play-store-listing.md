## Full description (max 4,000 characters)

```
Mantis is four precision calculators in one clean, private app: Basic, Scientific, Programmer and Unit Converter. No ads, no accounts, no internet permission – just fast, accurate answers.

BASIC
• Everyday arithmetic with brackets and correct order of operations
• Keep calculating from your last answer – results chain straight into the next sum
• Edit anywhere in your expression: tap to move the cursor, delete or insert
• Clear "Error" messages instead of silent wrong answers

SCIENTIFIC
• sin, cos, tan and their inverses (sin⁻¹, cos⁻¹, tan⁻¹)
• Degrees and radians
• Powers, square and cube roots, ln, log, eˣ, 10ˣ
• Factorial – including decimals via the gamma function (2.5! = 3.3233…)
• π and e at full precision
• Forget a closing bracket? Mantis closes it for you

PROGRAMMER
• HEX, DEC, OCT and BIN with instant base switching
• Bitwise AND, OR, XOR, NOT and bit shifts (<<, >>)
• Full 64-bit values, shown as real bit patterns in HEX, OCT and BIN
• Keys that are invalid for the current base are greyed out

UNIT CONVERTER
• Length, weight, temperature and number bases
• Live results as you type – no "=" needed
• Exact international definitions (1 mile = 1.609344 km, 1 lb = 0.45359237 kg)
• Negative temperatures and one-tap unit swap

PRECISION YOU CAN TRUST
• 15 significant digits, with scientific notation for very large and very small numbers
• No floating-point noise: 0.1 + 0.2 = 0.3
• Invalid input like 1.2.3 is blocked instead of producing a wrong result

DESIGNED FOR EVERY PHONE
• Light and dark themes that follow your system
• Large text sizes, split-screen and tablets supported
• Screen reader labels for every key
• Haptic feedback on every press

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Mantis has no internet permission, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your calculations never leave your device.
```
