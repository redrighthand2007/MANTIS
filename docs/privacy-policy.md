# Mantis Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 25 October 2026
**App:** Mantis – Precision Calculator (`com.kush.mantis`)
**Developer:** Kush

Mantis is a calculator and unit converter that works entirely on your device. This policy explains what that means for your data.

## Summary

Mantis does **not** collect, store, share or sell any personal data. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything off your device.

## Information we collect

None. Mantis does not collect:

- personal information such as your name, email address or phone number
- device identifiers, advertising IDs or location
- usage statistics, analytics or crash reports
- the numbers, expressions or results you enter

## How the app works

- **Calculations stay on your device.** Everything you type is processed locally and kept only in your phone's memory while the app is in use.
- **No internet access.** Mantis does not request the Android internet permission, so it cannot connect to any server.
- **No accounts.** You never need to sign up or sign in.
- **No ads and no third-party tracking.** Mantis contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.
- **Temporary state.** If Android closes the app in the background to free memory, it may briefly keep your current, unfinished calculation so it is still there when you return. This state is stored by Android on your device only, is never transmitted, and is cleared when you close the app.

## Permissions

Mantis requests no runtime permissions. It does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, files, location or network.

## Third-party components

Mantis uses open-source libraries (Android Jetpack and the mXparser math library) that run entirely inside the app. None of them collect or transmit data in Mantis.

## Children's privacy

Mantis is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data at all, it collects no data from children.

## Data deletion

There is no stored data to delete. Uninstalling Mantis removes the app and everything associated with it from your device.
